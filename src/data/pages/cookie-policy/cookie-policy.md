  <section>
    <h2>1. What Are Cookies?</h2>
    <p>
      Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.
      They help websites remember your preferences, recognize your device, and
      improve functionality.
    </p>
  </section>

  <p>Cookies may be:</p>

  <ul class="bullet-list">
    <li><strong>Session cookies</strong> (deleted when you close your browser)</li>
    <li><strong>Persistent cookies</strong> (stored for a set period of time)</li>
    <li><strong>First-party cookies</strong> (set by Ready Aim Retire)</li>
    <li><strong>Third-party cookies</strong> (set by service providers we use)</li>
  </ul>
